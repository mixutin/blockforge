# Accounts and sharing

1. Click **Sign in** → **Create account**. Choose a username (3–24 letters, digits or `_`), your email and a password (8+ characters).
2. We email you a link. Open it and press the button to **verify** your email (the link works for 24 hours; **Resend verification email** sends a new one, 3 per hour).
3. Without verifying you can still save projects privately. To **publish** or make **API tokens**, verify first.

**Save online:** **My projects** saves the current project to your account (up to 200 projects). **Save as new** makes a copy.

**Publish:** click **Share**, fill in title, description and notes, tick the box, click **Make public…** and then **Yes, publish now**. You get:

- a **link** (`/p?id=…`) anyone can open, play, "See inside" and **Remix**;
- an **embed code** (`<iframe src="…/embed/…">`) for other websites. Embeds only play the project;
- a **QR code**.

Your project also appears on **Explore**. **Unshare (revoke link)** turns the link, embeds and QR code off for good; sharing again makes a new link.

**Remix** copies a public project into your own account as a new private project called "Remix of …".

Shared projects always run in a sealed **sandboxed player**, so they can't read your account or files.
